About Me

Highly accomplished AI/ML Engineer with over 6 years of experience specializing in Natural Language Processing (NLP), Agentic AI, and Large Language Models (LLMs). Proven expertise in the end-to-end design, development, and deployment of scalable, production-ready AI solutions.

I thrive in async-first teams and have been working remotely with international companies since 2019. I'm currently the AI (NLP) Technical Lead at Intelligent Relations, where I lead the architecture of Preston, an AI-powered PR automation platform.

I'm based in Islamabad, Pakistan, and I'm also building Authr, a content-intelligence SaaS that crawls a brand's blog and competitor blogs, clusters content into a vector space, identifies coverage gaps, clones brand voice, and drafts SEO-linked posts.
